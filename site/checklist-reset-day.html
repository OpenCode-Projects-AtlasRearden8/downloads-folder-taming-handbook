Reset-Day Checklist

Your weekly ten minutes, step by step. Print this, pin it, or keep it open in a tab — sorting while reading the rules is faster than trusting your memory. Companion: the one-page Decision Card.

Set a timer for ten minutes before you start. The timer is the point. When it rings, you stop; whatever is left waits for next week, in the inbox box, guilt-free.

Before you start (30 seconds)

  • [ ] Timer set: 10 minutes, visible, loud-ish
  • [ ] Decision Card open on the other half of the screen
  • [ ] Know where the three homes are (Filing/Reference, Filing/Projects, Filing/Archive) and that Inbox-unsorted exists

Step 1 · Desktop first (2–3 min)

  • [ ] Every file on Desktop gets a destination: Reference / Projects / Archive / Trash / Inbox-unsorted (rules on the Decision Card)
  • [ ] Screenshot pile → pick any worth keeping, trash the rest of the session
  • [ ] Leftover "shortcut-looking" icons: shortcuts stay clipped to the sort — dead shortcuts (target deleted) → trash
  • [ ] Desktop count, finishing state: ≤ ~6 files (1–3 open projects only)

Step 2 · Downloads, oldest first (4–5 min)

Sort by date modified, oldest at top. The old stuff is the easiest to judge.

  • [ ] Kill all installers: every .exe, .msi, .dmg, .pkg, and already-installed/decided-against .zip → trash (keep the rare no-longer-downloadable one — see the Decision Card)
  • [ ] Die, duplicates: files ending in (1), -copy, -2, - FINAL → all but the newest → trash
  • [ ] Real keepers with search-flavor → Filing/Reference (rename the ones that matter, don't open them to look)
  • [ ] Deadline keepers → Filing/Projects (put the date in the name: due-2026-11-04 ...)
  • [ ] Confession keeps (ID scans, insurance, taxes) → Filing/Archive
  • [ ] Anything that stalls you > 5 seconds → Inbox-unsorted, no guilt

Step 3 · Inbox-unsorted (2 min max)

  • [ ] Chip away at last week's catch-all — same six gates from the Decision Card, but only while the timer is comfortable; two less than full is fine
  • [ ] Today's leftovers stay in Inbox-unsorted (that's its job)

Step 4 · Close out (1 min)

  • [ ] Empty Recycle Bin (or skip if you want a week's buffer — but then do it next week, actually)
  • [ ] Glance at Filing/Projects — anything whose deadline passed? Move to Reference or trash, fast
  • [ ] Write the win: one sentence, e.g. "Downloads at 23 items, was 87." That number is your future bait for next week.

You missed a week? Two? Six?

Not a failure — a different starting point. Chapter 6: The Rescue Plan is this same ritual in heavy armor, and it works at any depth of mess. Start wherever you are; the folder won't judge and neither do we.
